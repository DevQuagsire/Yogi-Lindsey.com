About

Presence in healing spaces

Lindsey brings yoga and sound healing into treatment and recovery centers, offering gentle, grounding practices that support people as they rebuild balance, breath, and a sense of safety in their bodies.

Through mindful movement, guided breath, and restorative sound, her work meets clients where they are—creating calm, connection, and tools for recovery that can travel beyond the mat and into everyday life.
